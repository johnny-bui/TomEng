## Editorial review still required

The 1,050 new phrases and their translations are an editorial draft. Automated checks cannot certify natural collocations, politeness, or translation nuance. A fluent business-English editor/native speaker should review the full set before treating it as final published learning content. TomEng's phrase detail includes a local-only draft editor for corrections; it does not silently modify canonical IDs or the source dataset.
